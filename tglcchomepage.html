About

Built for The Greatest Love of Christ Church

The TGLCC Church Management System is our church's digital record book — replacing scattered spreadsheets and paper logs with one trustworthy place to keep track of who belongs to our family, who serves and who leads.

Every member's story lives here: when they started attending, their baptism, the ministry they serve in and every Friday they've walked through our doors. It's built so pastors, admins and ministry heads can see the health of the church at a glance — and follow up with the people who need it most.

Access is protected by role-based login, so the right people see the right information, while the whole church body stays visible to those who shepherd it.

Why we built this

To know our members by name, not by number — and to never lose track of someone who stops showing up.

Services

Everything a shepherd's heart needs

Purpose-built tools for church administration — designed around how ministries actually run.

Member Records

Full profiles with photo, contact, address, birthday, inviter, and baptism history — searchable in seconds.

Benefit: no more lost paper forms

Attendance Tracking

Log every Friday service and instantly see who's present, who's missing, and who needs a follow-up call.

Benefit: catch dropped-off members early

Attendance Analytics

Month-by-month and year-by-year attendance trends, plus high/low week patterns, all visualized.

Benefit: data-driven ministry planning

Worker & Leader Tracking

Mark members as workers or leaders, record their commissioning dates, and celebrate serving anniversaries.

Benefit: honor faithful service

Church Structure & Org Chart

An always-current organizational chart built automatically from ministry roles assigned to each member.

Benefit: clarity on who reports to whom

Secure, Role-Based Access

Admin, staff, and viewer roles with password protection — plus Excel import/export for backups and reports.

Benefit: data stays safe and organized
